How To Annotate

You do not need research knowledge. Just judge what would feel natural and correct to a Korean reader.

1. Read the example

Look at the source sentence, the Korean reference, and the entity mention. Ask yourself what name or title a Korean reader would most naturally expect.

2. Decide the ideal Korean form

Before scoring the models, decide whether this entity should usually be translated, transliterated, preserved in English, or adapted.

3. Judge each model separately

For each model, decide whether the entity is right, what strategy it used, and whether the output feels acceptable in Korean.

4. Leave notes only when helpful

Use notes for cases like "official Korean title exists", "English should have been kept", or "this feels awkward but still understandable".
